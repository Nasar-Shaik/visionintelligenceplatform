- **AI-5c Inference Scheduling & Resource Management ✅ code+tests complete, ⏳ awaiting review** (plan approved + 8 + 7 further refinements; **25 recommendations total** folded in). **Stage 4 of the reference architecture — pre-specified, therefore implemented rather than invented**; **in `ai/inference`, no new service; frozen five untouched.** AI-5b protected a session from its own camera; **AI-5c protects sessions from each other.** **`AdmissionController`** estimates a session's cost and **refuses** unservable work with typed reasons (`session-capacity`/`compute-capacity`/`reserve-protected`/`no-compatible-resource` — different causes need different operator responses). **`InferenceScheduler`** uses **weighted-fair** rotation so priority shapes share **without permitting starvation** (`maxConsecutivePerSession` bounds any run even under `strict-priority`); round-robin/strict-priority and batching also available. **`ResourceGovernor`** walks an **ordered, reversible, hysteretic** ladder (`none→reduced-fps→reduced-resolution→reduced-behaviors→shedding-frames→suspended`) with a per-deployment **ceiling** (hospital never suspends) and **predictive** triggering from a least-squares queue trend — acting while headroom remains rather than after frames are lost. **Compute is hardware-independent**: `ComputeResource` uses **abstract dimensionless units** (never cores/VRAM) across cpu/cuda/tensorrt/openvino/metal/tpu/npu with **node-qualified ids** (`node-a/cuda:0`), so distributed scheduling later adds resources rather than a redesign; a **capacity reservation** (default 10%, critical-only) preserves the headroom a saturated runtime needs to recover from its own success. **Per-session accounting** (CPU/mem/GPU **attributed** from measured work, documented as estimates — one process has no per-session kernel accounting) + **`SessionSla`** (target vs actual FPS/latency, `met`, attainment %). **`AnalyzerCostModel`** degrades the most expensive analyzers first and **never** protected safety ones. **Seven portable deployment profiles** (retail/warehouse/office/school/hospital/factory/parking) — operational defaults as **config, not code**, deliberately distinct from the AI-4 `BehaviorProfile` (_how hard to work_ vs _what to detect_). Every action recorded as a structured **`SchedulerDecision`**. `CameraCapabilities` **extended additively** (fpsRange/streamProfiles/onvif) and **read instead of probed**. `RuntimeMetrics` partitioned **operational vs AI**. - **AI-5d Health Monitoring, Auto-Recovery & Model Lifecycle ✅ code+tests complete, ⏳ awaiting review** (plan approved + 8 further recommendations; **17 recommendations total** folded in). **In `ai/inference`, no new service; frozen five untouched; no new architectural layer** — AI-5d _supervises_ stages that already exist. AI-5b made a session survive its **camera**, AI-5c its **neighbours**; AI-5d makes it survive **itself over time**. **`HealthMonitor`** replaces the healthy/unhealthy bit with a **0–100 score decomposed across connection · inference · scheduler · resources · recovery** (every component **required** in the output — a report that omits a subsystem is the one that misdirects an operator), each indicator carrying a `Trend` so health reports where it is **heading**; a projected decline becomes one more pressure reason on the governor's existing path, because **health decides nothing** — two components able to degrade a session independently is how you get undebuggable oscillation. **`AutoRecovery`** **executes the frozen AI-5b taxonomy** within a **budget expressed as configuration**: retail 3 restarts · factory 10 · bank unlimited-with-long-cooldown · **healthcare never auto-restarts** — all four shipped as deployment profiles; `configuration` failures are **never retried**, enforced as a code-level invariant that _rejects_ a profile asking for it. **`RecoveryReason`** records trigger · subsystem · severity · retry count · correlation id, and **every refusal is recorded too**. **Progressive restoration** replaces ladder reversal: each degradation pushes a **`RestoreStep`** recording exactly what it removed and each recovery pops it, making exact-reverse restoration **structural rather than intended**. **Anti-oscillation** is **direction-aware** — a reversal waits out a stabilization window, but two escalations in a row are the ladder doing its job and are never delayed (this correction surfaced when a naive guard broke two existing scheduler tests). **`ModelLifecycleManager`** stages every version change `warming → validating → switching → draining → active` with rollback on the same mechanism run backwards; zero downtime rests on one indirection — the analyzer holds a **`ModelSlot`** read **per frame**, so promotion is an attribute assignment and no session stops. Validation is **operational, explicitly not accuracy** (artifact-loads · inference-runs · output-structure · latency-budget · detection-comparability); certifying recall needs labelled footage and is AI-5e. **History is append-only** — `versionPath: ['v1','v2','v1','v3','v4']` reads as "v2 was tried and rolled back" at a glance. **`DiagnosticsJournal`** merges admission · scheduling · degradation · recovery · restart · model · failure into one bounded tenant-scoped stream **as a sink on the log a session already writes to** (a second emitter would recreate the problem), rendered newest-first for machines and **oldest-first as a timeline** for humans. **Ten production-condition simulations** (prolonged operation · intermittent disconnects · burst reconnects · varying frame rates · heterogeneous hardware · mixed priorities · degraded hardware · repeated model failures · simultaneous recoveries · recovery storms) assert **invariants**, and the two load-bearing ones were **negative-controlled** — they fail when the mechanism is removed. Benchmark `--scenarios` folds them into the same accept/reject gate. Two corrections the work forced: the model-latency regression bound is **skipped below 1 ms/frame** (gating on timer noise is the opposite of an evidence gate), and a frozen test clock **hung** the connection supervisor's backoff.

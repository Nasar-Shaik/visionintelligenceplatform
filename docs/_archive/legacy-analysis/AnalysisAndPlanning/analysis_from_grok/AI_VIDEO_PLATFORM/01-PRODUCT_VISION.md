# 01-PRODUCT_VISION.md

## Purpose
Define the overarching vision for the AI Video Intelligence Platform.

## Vision Statement
An enterprise-grade, AI-powered video analytics platform that transforms raw video feeds from cameras into actionable intelligence, enabling proactive security, operational efficiency, and safety across diverse industries. It supports seamless cloud-edge hybrid deployments, real-time analysis at scale, and custom AI behaviors.

## Goals
- Detect complex events in real-time with high accuracy (>95% for core detections).
- Scale from 10 to 100,000+ cameras.
- Provide intuitive dashboards for non-technical users.
- Ensure privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA).
- Support offline edge operations with sync.

## Key Differentiators
- Hybrid edge-cloud with model optimization (TensorRT, ONNX).
- Custom behavior rules via no-code interface.
- Multi-modal AI (video + metadata).
- Cost-effective at petabyte scale.

## Target Market
As per query: Small shops to large enterprises and government.

## Future
Drone integration, predictive analytics, generative AI for scene reconstruction.
